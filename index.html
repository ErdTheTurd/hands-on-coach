Piano

Finger numbers, then songs.

Each lesson highlights a key and places a ghost finger on it. Early lessons cover finger numbers and hand position, then left-hand basics, scales, chords, and songs.

Free Play keeps live fingering hints on screen while you explore. Practice on a real keyboard, a paper keyboard, or the virtual keyboard in the app.

Guitar, knots, and ASL use the same coaching.

Guitar includes six beginner chord lessons. Ghost fingers mark frets on a virtual fretboard. The microphone checks that the chord is ringing.

Knots covers five common knots. Each step has an illustration and grip poses so you can see where both hands go before you tighten the rope.

ASL fingerspelling teaches the alphabet, A to Z, across seven lessons. A ghost hand holds the letter shape. On-device hand pose checking confirms when your handshape matches. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Practice without an instrument.

Demo Mode uses virtual instruments, so you can complete every lesson without a piano, guitar, or camera. It is on by default the first time you open the app.

Daily goals, streaks, and ten achievements stay on the device. Optional reminders are scheduled locally. Free Play shows fingering hints with no score attached.

Apple Vision Pro

A floating 3D piano in the room.

On Vision Pro, place a floating keyboard in your space and practice with the same ghost-hand coaching. Hand tracking stays on the device.
