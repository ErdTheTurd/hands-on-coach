Hands-On Coach

Glowing ghost hands show you where your fingers go.

Learn piano, guitar chords, knots, and ASL fingerspelling. A translucent hand sits over the keys, the fretboard, the rope, or your own hand. You match the glow.

Piano

The finger, not just the key.

Most lesson apps name the note. This one shows which finger to use, and where to put it.

Start with finger numbers and hand position, then left-hand basics, scales, chords, and songs. Free Play follows along with live fingering hints.

Use a real keyboard, a paper keyboard, or the built-in virtual one.

More skills

Guitar, knots, and sign, coached the same way.

The ghost hands are not only for piano. Each skill uses the same idea: see the shape, match it, and keep going.

Guitar

Six beginner chord lessons. Ghost fingers mark the frets. The microphone checks that the chord is actually ringing.

Knots

Five essential knots, one step at a time. Illustrations and grip poses show you where each hand goes.

ASL fingerspelling

The full alphabet, A to Z, across seven lessons. The camera checks your handshape on the device and tells you when it matches.

Practice

Practice without the hardware.

Demo Mode and Free Play

No piano or guitar nearby? Demo Mode lets you try every lesson with virtual instruments. You do not need a camera or a real instrument. In Free Play, ghost hands stay with you and hint at the fingering as you go.

Daily goals and achievements

Daily goals, streaks, and ten achievements. Optional reminders stay on your phone. Your progress is stored on the device, nowhere else.

Apple Vision Pro

A floating 3D piano in your room.

On Vision Pro, practice happens in an immersive space. The same ghost-hand coaching is there; the keyboard just sits in the room with you.

Privacy

Nothing is recorded. Nothing is uploaded.

The camera, microphone, and hand tracking are processed on your device only. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.

Hands-On Coach works without the internet. Progress stays on the device you are using. Read the full privacy policy.

The short version

Free, and it stays on the device.

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy in the app. The camera and microphone are used only to check your fingers and your notes, and only if you turn those features on.

You can decline those permissions and still use Demo Mode with virtual instruments.
