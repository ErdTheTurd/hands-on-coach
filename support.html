Support

Fingerwiz is a free app for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro. It is not on the App Store yet. If you need help, try the questions below. Or email me at support@fingerwiz.com.

What is Demo Mode?
Demo Mode lets you try every lesson with virtual instruments. You do not need a real piano, guitar, camera, or microphone. It is on by default when you first open the app, so you can go through a lesson right away.
Why does the app ask for camera or microphone access?
Only if you use live checking. The microphone can check piano notes and guitar chords. The camera and on-device hand pose can check piano fingering, knot grips, and ASL fingerspelling. That work happens on your device only. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. You can say no to these permissions and keep using Demo Mode.
How do I reset my progress?
Progress is stored only on that device. There is no account and no cloud backup. Removing the app from the device deletes that local progress. Installing it again starts you over on that device.
Does it work on Apple Vision Pro?
Yes. On Vision Pro, open the Piano skill and start practice in your space to place a floating 3D piano. Hand tracking is used only on the device to detect key presses. The same ghost-hand coaching is there.
Is my data uploaded somewhere?
No. There is no account. There are no ads. There is no tracking. Progress stays on your device. For the details, see the privacy policy.

Contact

Email support@fingerwiz.com and say which device you are using (iPhone, iPad, or Vision Pro) and what you were trying to do. I will get back to you as soon as I can.
