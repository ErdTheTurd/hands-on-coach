Support

Fingerwiz is a free app for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro. It is not on the App Store yet. For help, review the questions below or email support@fingerwiz.com.

What is Demo Mode?
Demo Mode lets you complete every lesson with virtual instruments. You do not need a real piano, guitar, camera, or microphone. It is on by default when you first open the app.
Why does the app ask for camera or microphone access?
Only for live checking. The microphone can check piano notes and guitar chords. The camera and on-device hand pose can check piano fingering, knot grips, and ASL fingerspelling. That work happens on your device only. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. You can decline these permissions and keep using Demo Mode.
How do I reset my progress?
Progress is stored only on that device. There is no account and no cloud backup. Removing the app deletes that local progress. Installing again starts over on that device.
Does it work on Apple Vision Pro?
Yes. On Vision Pro, open the Piano skill and start practice in your space to place a floating 3D piano. Hand tracking is used only on the device to detect key presses. The same ghost-hand coaching is available.
Is my data uploaded somewhere?
No. There is no account, and progress stays on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

Contact

Email support@fingerwiz.com with your device (iPhone, iPad, or Vision Pro) and what you were trying to do.
