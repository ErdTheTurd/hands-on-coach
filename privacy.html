Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Fingerwiz is made by Edward Dunn. This policy describes how the iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro app handles information. The app does not collect data and does not send it anywhere.

What we collect

We do not collect data from this app. There is no account, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Camera, microphone, and hand tracking

Some lessons can use the camera, the microphone, or hand tracking to check fingering, handshape, or whether a chord is in tune:

  • The microphone can check piano notes and guitar chords.
  • The camera and on-device hand pose can check piano fingering, knot grips, and ASL fingerspelling handshapes.
  • On Apple Vision Pro, hand tracking can detect key presses on the floating 3D piano.

This information is processed on your device only, in real time. It is not recorded, stored off the device, or uploaded. You can decline these permissions and still use Demo Mode with virtual instruments.

Progress and reminders

Lesson progress, streaks, daily goals, and achievements are stored locally on the device you are using. They do not sync to a server or across devices.

Optional practice reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent by us.

Internet and accounts

The app works without an internet connection and without an account. There is no login, and there is no backend that receives lesson data.

Children

Fingerwiz is a general-audience app. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This website

This marketing site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use analytics cookies and does not require an account. Support email is handled as ordinary email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@fingerwiz.com.
